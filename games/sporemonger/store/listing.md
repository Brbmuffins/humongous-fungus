## Description (4000)

```
Buy low. Sell before it rots.

You run a tiny stall at the night market of a mushroom town, with 250 caps and an empty cellar. Prices change every 3 seconds, day and night, even while the app is closed. Time it right and your stall grows. Time it wrong and your morels turn to mush.

A MARKET THAT NEVER SLEEPS
Six goods, each with its own personality: windfall apples that swing wildly and rot in hours, honey that drifts slowly, morels that boom and crash, pricey truffles that are slow to turn, glowcaps with the wildest price in town, and forest amber for the very rich. Every good has a live chart that marks what you paid.

EVERYTHING ROTS
Most goods spoil while you hold them, even while you're away. Sell before they turn, or the compost cart takes them for a tenth of their value.

LISTEN TO THE GOSSIP
When a price is about to turn, the town sometimes talks about it. "The duke wants morels for his wedding feast." Rumors can be wrong. A better gossip network hears more of them, and more of them true.

COME BACK TO NEWS
Close the app and the market keeps moving. When you return, see what your stall is worth now, what rose, what fell and what rotted. Get a reminder when something you hold is up.

CLIMB FROM BASKET PEDDLER TO SPORE BARON
Grow your net worth to unlock new goods and ranks. Spend caps on a bigger cellar, cool stones that slow the rot, a gossip network, and haggling that cuts the market's fee.

NEVER STUCK
Short on caps? Tap the woods to forage. A bad run never ends the game.

- Plays fully offline
- No ads, no accounts, no data collected
- No real money: caps are pretend, and so is the market
- Made by one person at Muffin Development
```

## App Review notes

```
Sporemonger is an offline trading game. No account or login is needed.
Tap a good in the Trade tab, then buy and sell with the buttons under its
chart. Caps are a pretend in-game currency: they cannot be bought with real
money or exchanged for anything, and all prices are generated by the game.
Local notifications are optional and only remind the player when a good
they hold has gone up in price or is rotting.
```
