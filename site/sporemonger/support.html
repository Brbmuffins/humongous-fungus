Common questions

Do prices really move while the app is closed?
Yes. Prices change every 3 seconds, day and night, and stock you hold keeps rotting. When you come back, the game plays out the time you were away (up to a week).

How do I get reminders?
Allow notifications the first time you buy something, or turn them on later in your phone's Settings under Sporemonger. The game reminds you when something you hold is up, or half rotten.

I ran out of caps. Is my game over?
Never. Tap the woods at the top of the screen to forage for a few caps at any time.

Is any of this real money?
No. Caps are a pretend currency and the market is made up. Nothing can be bought or cashed out.

Is my progress backed up?
Progress is saved on your device only. Deleting the app deletes your stall.

How do I start over?
Open the Stall tab and choose Erase save.
