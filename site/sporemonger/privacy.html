Sporemonger

Privacy policy · Effective October 13, 2026 · Muffin Development

The short version

Sporemonger does not collect, store or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your device

Your game progress (your caps, stock, upgrades, rumors and statistics) is saved only on your device, using the phone's own storage. It is never sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app deletes it.

No real money

Caps are a pretend currency inside the game. They cannot be bought, sold or exchanged for anything, and the market is made up: prices come from the game itself, not from any real market.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the game schedules a reminder on your device, for example when something you are holding has gone up in price or is about to rot. These are local notifications: they are created on your phone and involve no server. You can turn them off at any time in your phone's settings.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, for example because a future version adds an optional feature that needs data, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the effective date above will change.
