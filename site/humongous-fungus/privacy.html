Humongous Fungus

Privacy policy · Effective October 13, 2026 · Muffin Development

The short version

Humongous Fungus does not collect, store or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your device

Your game progress (your jar, upgrades, specimens and statistics) is saved only on your device, using the phone's own storage. It is never sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app deletes it.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the game schedules reminders on your device, for example when a specimen you planted has finished growing. These are local notifications: they are created on your phone and involve no server. You can turn them off at any time in your phone's settings.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, for example because a future version adds an optional feature that needs data, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the effective date above will change.
