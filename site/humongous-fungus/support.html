Common questions

Does the jar grow while the app is closed?
Yes, up to your away limit (2 hours at first). Dormancy upgrades in the Evolve tab and the Ghost pipe specimen raise it.

How do I get reminders?
Allow notifications when you plant your first specimen, or turn them on later in your phone's Settings under Humongous Fungus.

Is my progress backed up?
Progress is saved on your device only. Deleting the app deletes your jar.

How do I start over?
Open the Journal tab and choose Erase save.
