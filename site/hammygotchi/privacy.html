Hammygotchi

Privacy policy · Effective October 27, 2026 · Muffin Development

The short version

Hammygotchi does not collect, store or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your device

Your game (Hammy, his needs, your coins, toys and fits, his texts and the graveyard) is saved only on your device, using the phone's own storage. It is never sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app deletes it.

His phone is pretend

The texts and calls in the game are made up by the game. They don't use your phone's real calling or messaging, your contacts, or your phone number. What you type or say to him is answered by the game itself, on your device, and never leaves the app. His voice is your device's own built-in speech. If your device supports it, speaking to him on a call uses its own speech recognition, which may be handled by your phone or browser maker.

No real money

Hammy coins are a pretend currency inside the game. They cannot be bought, sold or exchanged for anything. DoorDosh is a joke inside the game: no food is ordered and no payment is taken.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the game schedules reminders on your device, for example Hammy's next text or a warning when he is starving. These are local notifications: they are created on your phone and involve no server. You can turn them off at any time in your phone's settings.

Children

The game collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, for example because a future version adds an optional feature that needs data, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the effective date above will change.
