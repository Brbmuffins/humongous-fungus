Common questions

Does Hammy keep living while the app is closed?
Yes. He gets hungry, sad and stinky in real time, texts you, calls you, and orders food on your coins when he's hungry. When you come back, the game plays out the time you were away.

How long until he dies?
About a day of complete neglect, starting from full. Feed him, scoop his poop, and text or call him a couple of times a day and he'll be fine. Probably.

Why is he a meatball sub today?
Hammy changes every day at midnight: some days a hamster, some days a meatball sub, some days a train. Hats and glasses still go on; shirts and costumes wait for hamster days.

How do I get reminders?
Allow notifications the first time you order him food, or turn them on later in your phone's Settings under Hammygotchi.

He died. Is my game over?
You lose, but you can get a new Hammy. Your fits and toys stay yours; coins start over.

Is any of this real money or real food?
No. Hammy coins are pretend, and DoorDosh is a joke. Nothing can be bought or cashed out.

How do I start over completely?
Open the Life tab and choose Erase save.
