# Humongous Fungus 1.0.0

The first release. A cozy idle game where decay is the engine: you are the
fungus in a sealed terrarium, and everything that grows in the jar eventually
falls back into the soil and feeds you.

## What's in it

- Tap the jar to digest leaf litter, then grow eight producers that digest
  for you, from your own hyphae to dyeing dart frogs. Milestones at 25, 50,
  100 and more of each double their output.
- Evolve one-time upgrades: species boosts, partnerships, stronger taps and
  longer time away.
- Cultivate six rare specimens that take real time to grow (5 minutes to 24
  hours) for permanent bonuses.
- Seal the jar at 10B nutrients to release spores (+10% production each,
  forever) and start again in a new biome.
- Time away counts: the jar keeps growing while the game is closed, and the
  welcome-back screen shows what it earned.
- Golden blooms every few minutes for a ×7 frenzy, and a journal of 14
  species with their real Latin names.

## Play it

Download `humongous-fungus-1.0.0.html` below and open it in any browser, on a
phone or a computer. It's one self-contained file and works offline. Your
jar is saved in that browser.

## Phone apps

The iOS and Android apps are built by the `ios-humongous-fungus` and
`android-humongous-fungus` workflows: run them by hand from the Actions tab
to sign and upload to TestFlight and Google Play. This release is version
1.0.0; build numbers count up on their own. The steps for the stores are in
`docs/app-store-checklist.md`.
