# Last Muffin Standing 1.0.0

The first release. A survivors game in a bakery: you are a muffin on the
floor after closing, and everything that eats muffins is coming for you. You
only move; your weapons fire on their own. Survive until 6 AM.

## What's in it

- 12-minute nights from midnight to sunrise, then overtime if you want more.
- Eight weapons, from the Blueberry Blaster to the Frosting Bomb, each with an
  evolution, and nine passives including Big Bonk and the Sous Chef cupcakes
  that fetch sprinkles for you.
- BONK: every hit has a chance to triple its damage and send the pest flying.
- Pests from ants to raccoons and seagulls, rings that close in, flocks that
  fly across, and three bosses: the Queen Ant, the Rat King and the Toaster.
- Seven muffins to play, each with a starting weapon and a perk.
- The Pantry: upgrades bought with crumbs between nights, including
  automation (the Fridge Magnet, a Recipe Book that picks level-ups for you,
  and Auto-Hop).
- A game speed setting (Normal, Fast and Turbo; Fast by default), and a
  smooth frame rate on slower phones.

## Play it

Download `last-muffin-standing-1.0.0.html` below and open it in any browser,
on a phone or a computer. It's one self-contained file and works offline.
Drag anywhere to move (or WASD and the arrow keys); Space hops. Your
crumbs and Pantry are saved in that browser.

## Phone apps

Not in the stores yet. The Capacitor config and phone features (durable
saves, haptics, pausing in the background) are ready in `games/lastmuffin`;
add the native projects with `npx cap add ios` and `npx cap add android`
when it's time.
