# Ticker Rush 1.0.0

The first release. Day trading as an arcade game: a whole session, 9:30 to
4:00, in about three minutes, on a make-believe market. Read the news, go
long or short in a tap, and be flat by the bell. Every company is made up,
and so is the money.

## What's in it

- Five tickers a day from twelve made-up food companies, from Maple Syrup
  Power & Light (nothing ever happens) to Muffin Top Holdings (the meme stock)
  and Dough Coin.
- A live chart that scrolls tick by tick, with 1- and 5-minute candles a tap
  away, a ticker crawl across the top, and prices that flash as they tick.
- Breaking news that moves prices a moment after it hits. Rumors get denied,
  good earnings sometimes get sold, and some news was already priced in.
- Trends to ride, volatility halts that reopen with a jump, opening gappers,
  meme-stock squeezes and a busy power hour.
- Long or short on 2× margin, real spreads and commissions, and a risk desk
  that takes your keys if you lose 10% in a day.
- A career: ranks from Intern to Market legend, a daily goal, grades, streaks
  and records.
- Tools bought with your account cash: bracket orders, a Level 2 tape that
  shows whales before a trend, a news squawk, a scanner, cheaper fees and
  more margin.
- Session length: Blitz (2 minutes), Normal (3) or Chill (5).

## Play it

Download `ticker-rush-1.0.0.html` below and open it in any browser, on a
phone or a computer. It's one self-contained file and works offline. On a
keyboard: B buys, S sells or shorts, X closes, F flattens everything, 1–4 pick
the size and the arrows switch stocks. Your career is saved in that browser.

## Phone apps

Not in the stores yet. The Capacitor config and phone features (durable
saves, haptics, pausing in the background) are ready in `games/tickerrush`;
add the native projects with `npx cap add ios` and `npx cap add android`
when it's time.
