# Sporemonger 1.0.0

The first release. A trading game in the night market of a mushroom-folk
town: start with 250 caps and an empty stall, buy low, sell high, and sell
before your stock rots.

## What's in it

- Six goods with live charts, each with its own personality: jumpy windfall
  apples, slow-cycling honey, morels that boom and crash, long-trending black
  truffles, wild glowcaps and very slow forest amber.
- Prices move every 3 seconds, day and night, even while the game is closed.
  The market keeps 3% of every sale, so tiny wiggles don't pay.
- Most goods rot while you hold them, also while you're away. Rotten stock
  goes to the compost cart for a tenth of its value.
- Town gossip hints when a price is about to turn. Rumors can be wrong.
- Ranks by net worth unlock new goods, from Stall keeper to Spore baron.
- Stall upgrades: a bigger cellar, cool stones, a gossip network, haggling
  and a foraging basket.
- Forage by tapping the banner when you're short, so a bad run never ends the
  game.

## Play it

Download `sporemonger-1.0.0.html` below and open it in any browser, on a
phone or a computer. It's one self-contained file and works offline. Your
stall is saved in that browser.

## Phone apps

The iOS and Android apps are built by the `ios-sporemonger` and
`android-sporemonger` workflows: run them by hand from the Actions tab to sign
and upload to TestFlight and Google Play. This release is version 1.0.0;
build numbers count up on their own. The steps for the stores are in
`docs/app-store-checklist.md`.
